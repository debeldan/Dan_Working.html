Thank you for downloading the 2026 Parking Structure Cost Outlook. I wanted to send a quick note while it’s still fresh.

The headline number — $33,300 per space — is a useful starting point, but it’s almost never the number you should actually budget against. Two things move it before a drawing is issued: where you’re building, and what isn’t included in the median.

The first gap is location. The 34-metro index in the back of the paper exists precisely because the same parking structure prices very differently market to market. The second gap is what “hard cost” leaves out — design and engineering fees, geotechnical work, materials testing, special inspections, permitting, and financing — which together typically add another 15 to 25 percent on top of the construction cost.

The case in the paper

Houston came in lowest this year at $27,806 per space. San Francisco came in highest at $42,624. That’s a $14,818 spread — roughly 53 percent — on the same building type. Which is why a project sized off the national median alone, without a location factor, can be off by tens of thousands per stall before a foundation is poured.
